## 11/6/2025 7 AM - Started the schematic  

_Time spent: 2.0h_  

I was looking through some reddit posts and found that someone had a cool looking PCB ruler and I decided to make my own (to take a break from other thought intensive projects) and yea...

I started by researching the most common footprints and packages to include in the PCB and also a list of stuff that I plan on adding:

- AWG Guage / Via hole size
- Trace Thickness
- Ruler with in/mm (duh)
- Protractor
- Silkscreen with MOSFET channel types
- Silkscreen with formulas
- Silkscreen with Smith Chart :skull:

## 11/11/2025 - NFC, Reduced Price, and Blender  

_Time spent: 5.0h_  

Changed the shipping to GSDL to reduce the price and I also wanted to add a bit more complexity so I included NFC. As this field interested me after making a hacker card, I wanted to learn more about the technology so I read about how I could make my own NFC antenna and read through this datasheet:

![image](https://blueprint.hackclub.com/user-attachments/blobs/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTAyMDEsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--7b3befccad7f9ec5bc28b28aaaf5b6c15750b01f/image.png)
